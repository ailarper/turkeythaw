# Product Context: Turkey Thaw Clock

## 1. Vision & Purpose
Turkey Thaw Clock is a fast, high-utility holiday cooking companion and interactive thaw timing calculator. It eliminates the single biggest Thanksgiving and Christmas cooking panic: calculating exactly when a frozen turkey must begin thawing to be safely ready for roasting day.

## 2. Target Audience
- Home cooks, hosts, and holiday planners facing tight kitchen schedules and food safety anxiety.
- Mobile-first visitors seeking instant, unambiguous answers without navigating recipe blog fluff.

## 3. Core Utility Features
- **Dual-Method Thaw Engine:**
  - *Refrigerator Thawing:* Standard safe method (~24 hours per 4-5 lbs at 40°F / 4°C).
  - *Cold Water Thawing:* Fast method (~30 minutes per lb with water rotated every 30 mins).
- **Target Meal Countdown:** User selects turkey weight and target cooking time/date; calculator outputs the precise timestamp and day to begin thawing.
- **Food Safety Checkpoints:** Integrated USDA food safety reminders, temperature thresholds (danger zone 40°F–140°F), and readiness warnings.
- **Curated Hardware Recommendations (Monetization):**
  - Seamlessly integrated product cards: Digital Instant-Read Meat Thermometer, Heavy-Duty Roasting Pan with V-Rack, Carving Knife Set, Oven Temperature Probe.
  - Zero disruptive popups or spam banners; high-intent affiliate recommendations only.

## 4. Engineering & Experience Standards
- **Core Web Vitals:** Strict 0 CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift), LCP < 1.2s.
- **Mobile First:** Instant responsiveness on all viewports without layout reflows.
- **Interactive State:** Zero external heavy dependencies; pure native reactive calculations.
